# Step 1 — remake the photo as a cinematic studio portrait

Use any image model that accepts a reference image: ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney (with `--cref`), Magnific, Flux, and others. Attach the user's photo as the reference, paste the prompt below, and ask for a 9:16 image at 2K or larger.

Why this step: the line drawing in step 2 draws lines where there is light. A flat phone photo or a busy background gives grey mush. A hard side light with a bright rim on a black background gives every hair and every curve of the face a line to follow.

```
Re-shoot the person from the reference as a tight cinematic studio close-up: the face fills about 70% of the frame, cropped at the upper chest, camera straight-on at eye level. Same exact face, skin tone and hair as the reference — no beautification, no changes to features. Pure black seamless background. Dramatic chiaroscuro: one hard side key light sculpts half of the face, the other half falls into deep shadow with only a whisper of fill. A thin bright cool-white rim light traces the hair, the jaw, the ear, the neck and the shoulders, and makes every hair strand glow against the black. Black turtleneck. Calm, confident expression, eyes just past the lens. Ultra-photorealistic, extreme detail: skin pores, individual hair strands, crisp eyelashes; 85mm f/2, high dynamic range, cinematic grade. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

Adapt it to the photo:
- Name what must stay the same, for example "wavy hair and hoop earrings" or "the beard".
- Change the clothing line to match the person, for example "black blazer over a white top".
- If the face changes, say "same exact face" again and regenerate. Never turn someone into a different person.

Check: the result should be a black background, a close-up, one lit side and one shadow side, and a bright rim. If it is not, regenerate before going on.
